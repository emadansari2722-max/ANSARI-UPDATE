MOBILE SE USE (SAME WiFi)
----------------------------------------
1. Panel PC par chalao (ANSARI.bat / command)
2. PC aur phone SAME WiFi par hon
3. Phone browser mein kholo:

   http://PC_IP:5000

   PC_IP kaise pata karein:
   • PC par CMD: ipconfig → "IPv4 Address" (jaise 192.168.1.5)
   • Ya PC browser: http://127.0.0.1:5000/lan-info

   Example mobile URL:
   http://192.168.1.5:5000

4. Login karo — same as PC
5. Connect / toggles phone se bhi chalenge

NOTE: Audio/video sounds optional hain — cheat ke liye zaroori nahi.
      Agar sound na chale mobile par, koi problem nahi.
